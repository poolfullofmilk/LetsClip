# LetsClip, Text And Layout

Every wording, spacing, colour, radius and shadow rule the owner picked. Read before touching any page, component, the stylesheet or the page script. Part of `CLAUDE.md`, its rules bind the same way.

## Text

- Labels are short Title Case, and inside one the short joining words stay lower case, the owner's pick: a, an, the, and, or, as, at, by, for, in, is, of, on, per, to, with (Start with Windows, Show in Folder, LetsClip v2.0.1 is Out; `is` joined the list on 9 November 2026, when a capital Is read as not clean). The first and last word keep their capital. Comments keep every word capitalised, RazorStyle checks that.
- Longer sentences (descriptions, hints, warnings, snackbars) are sentence case, short, and never end in a symbol; with two sentences only the last goes without a full stop.
- Text comes in five sizes since 21 November 2026, the owner's picks 2 and 3 of that day's sweep: 12 px for chips, captions and tooltips (`caption`; tooltips read 13 px before), 14 px for rows, values, every button whatever its size and every field's text (`body2`; fields read MudBlazor's 16 px and small buttons 13 px before, Cut Start and Cut End alone 14 px), 16 px for the navigation and the header counts, 20 px for group headers and 24 px for page titles. Every outlined field stays 40 px tall (`--field-height`, which the compact rows and the player's controls line share).
- The info glyph is `AppTheme.InfoIcon` everywhere, a grey `Notes` icon, the owner's pick over Material's circled i and over light blue: the About item, Info Notices and Info snackbars. Cards have none since 13 November 2026, their facts sit in their menu (see `Docs/History.md`, Layout: Tooltips, Card Facts And Finishing Touches). Explanations are not captions: a row that needs one is renamed until it does not, the owner removed Size Limit's.
- Every alert is a `Notice` (dense, raised, rounded, warnings in their own colour), and CSS sets its text to the body2 size of the text around it. Its 20 px icon sits 16 px from the left edge, the owner's pick, and its text is centred in the same width (`text-wrap: balance`, so two lines read evenly). The export's hint and its result name centre too.
- A value about a choice sits greyed (`mud-text-secondary`) on the right where the row has room, never in brackets: a monitor's size and refresh rate in its select item, a device's adapter beside its name, Recommended beside the recommended capture choice, the export size. Every item is an `ItemLabel` (its text never gives way, the detail ends in dots), and the closed select shows the same detail and colour through `FieldDetail` (a greyed `data-detail` on the right, the text amber or red like its item), so a closed field reads like its open item. Devices are named the Windows way, the owner's pick after `Intel® Smart Sound Technologie voor digitale microfoons` stood in for a microphone: the endpoint name (Microphone Array, Headphones, Luidsprekers) with the adapter greyed beside it, trademark marks taken out (`AudioDevices.NamesOf`, with a self test).
- The System Audio select starts with Game and Voice Chat and a line, and both audio selects go on with Automatic (`AudioDevices.AutomaticId`, an empty id that follows the Windows default device, for the microphone too), then a line (`divided` on that item: a `MudDivider` inside a `MudSelect` also renders under the field), then the devices. A Rounded check (`ItemLabel.Checked`, a hidden one keeps every name in line) marks the picked item and, while Automatic is picked, the device it follows too, the owner's pick, so Automatic carries no greyed device name, in the list or the closed field. A saved device that is gone reads Automatic and records through the default (`AudioMixer` falls back, also when the device is pulled out mid session) until it returns, then takes over again; the saved id stays. Both selects and the Noise Cancellation switch always show, disabled while their switch is off, never hidden. Test Microphone (left of the microphone select) opens the picked microphone, or the default, in `MicrophoneMeter` and draws its level along the bottom of the select (`LevelMeter`, its own 20 renders a second). It is live, the owner's pick over a first version that recorded five seconds and played them back: the microphone goes through an FFmpeg running the filters a clip gets (`CaptureSession.VoiceCleaning`, only with Noise Cancellation on) at the volume a clip opens at (`UserSettings.OpeningVolume`) straight to the Windows output, until Stop Test, leaving the page, turning the microphone off or a lost device, which ends it with a Notice (`Ended`). Changing the device or Noise Cancellation restarts it at once. The system audio loopback is muted for its whole length, renewed each second so a session that restarts stays muted, so no clip holds the test. A snackbar says headphones avoid an echo, since on speakers it can feed back. Measured on the real filter chain: speech comes out about 110 ms after it goes in (63 ms without cleaning, FFmpeg's low delay flags changed nothing), plus 20 ms of microphone and 30 ms of output buffer. Clock drift cannot build up delay: past 120 ms buffered it catches up (`s_maximumLag`). On `aevalsrc` speech at -35 dB over pink noise at -48 dB the word comes out at -40.9 dB and the gap at -65.5 dB with cleaning, -40.7 and -53.6 dB without, the same at 44.1 kHz, and the first second is as clean as the rest. Three seconds of pure silence say so, since on the owner's i5 a microphone that was not muted in Windows sent only zeros (-113 dB), which is what Windows' privacy setting or a mute key does. A switch whose list is empty cannot be turned on, only off.
- Settings and panel rows are a title and their control, no description under them (the owner's pick); the capture and export panels use the same `SettingRow`, whose `Switch`, `SwitchChanged` and `SwitchDisabled` put a switch at the row's end, named by the row's title. A row's title never gives way (`flex-shrink-0`) and pushes the rest right with an auto margin, so a field that should fill the row (Name) can; its value does give way, in dots. The status chip in the app bar names the game it records (Recording Overwatch), and while it records its tooltip says how much a save holds right now (`Holds the Last 42s`, `ReplayRecorder.HeldSeconds`, counting up each second while the replay fills after a start, the owner's pick 20 of 21 November 2026's sweep); it has no click unless it points at a fix: Waiting for a Game or App is an `action-chip` whose tooltip Edit Games and Apps opens that dialog, and Recording Error is one whenever only the person can fix it (`CaptureStatus.Fix`: Open Display Settings for HDR, Open Storage Settings for space, Pick Another Monitor for a monitor gone, Download LetsClip Again for a missing part), Try Again staying beside it (`CaptureChip`, since 20 November 2026). Every panel holds its content 24 px from its edge (`px-6`), cards 16 px (`pa-4`).
- No full path on screen: the clips folder shows relative to the profile (`Videos\LetsClip`), whole only when it lies outside it. The empty Clips page (`NoClips`, its own component so only it follows the capture state) reads No Clips Yet, then what records right now (`Recording Overwatch in the background`, `Recording Monitor 1 in the background`, or `Records in the background once one of your 17 games and apps starts`, the count those switched on), then `Press F10 to save what just happened`, or the amber `F10 is Taken` chip opening Settings when another app holds the key, and Edit Games and Apps while Games and Apps Only is on, since 20 November 2026 (it said `Press F10 To Save One` even while the key was taken). Its icon is grey like No Games Found in the Games and Apps dialog (`EmptyState` defaults to grey), the owner's pick over blue.
- No app is named: exports are ready to share and copied files paste anywhere. The one exception is About, which shows the GitHub repository description word for word, the owner's pick (since 9 November 2026 `Keeps the last moments of your game, app or monitor in the background, saves them with a hotkey and exports clips small enough for Discord`, where it said one monitor; the GitHub description must read the same), beside the version, the author as `Poolfullofmilk` with a capital P (`AppInfo.Author`, the owner's pick; the csproj's `Company` keeps the account name) and one Third Party Notices button.
- The monitor overlay names a monitor as `Display 1: 1920 × 1080` (`MonitorInfo.Label`), the same words the logs use.
- Numbers follow the Windows format (`Formatter` uses the current culture). Every shown date goes through `Formatter.FormatDateTime`, the owner's pick: `Today at 8:30` and `Yesterday at 12:16`, older ones `6th of November at 8:42`, with the year only when it is not this one (`6th of November 2025 at 8:42`), since 17 November 2026 (`6-11-2026 at 8:42` before; 24 hour time without a leading zero). Every shown time and length goes through `Formatter.FormatDuration`, zero parts left out (`8m30,5s`, `1m`, `0s`), the player clock and replay lengths too. A whole number never shows `,0`, the owner's rule for every number: `30 Mbps`, `20 MB`, `2 GB`, beside `30,5 Mbps` and `1,25 GB` (`FormatSize` with `0.#` and `0.##`, `FormatBitrate` with `0.#`, `formatSize` in the page script alike). A saved clip's file is named after its game, app or monitor and the press, `Overwatch 2026-11-14 21-05-33.mp4`, the owner's pick of 14 November 2026 so a shared file says what it holds (it was `LetsClip 2026-11-14 21-05-33`); the library reads the date back from the stamp (`ClipNames.DateOf`, any source before it), and an export is named like its clip's file. A given name renames the file to it (see `Docs/History.md`, Clip Files Carry Their Names). A clip's title is its given name, else Untitled (`ClipInfo.Title`, `DefaultTitle`); what it recorded is its `Source`, the game or app, else the monitor by its place in the monitor list, Monitor 1 or Monitor 2 (`ClipMetadata.Monitor`, set by `CaptureController.SaveAsync`; older clips read Monitor), the word the chips and the filter use; a copied file without a generated name shows its file name. Its date and size follow on one row, on the cards too.
- The capture settings are rows of Settings' Recording panel (see `Docs/History.md`, Layout: Clips Get The Room, Evening): Use Recommended Settings left on 13 November 2026, the owner's ask: Reset to Default sits at the right of the Settings title instead (a small red text button like every Reset, on the title's centre line and the panels' right edge, measured), disabled while every value is at its default and asking first. Recommended still marks the recommended choices in their selects. To look at monitor mode in a sandbox without recording, rename the build's `FFmpeg\ffmpeg.exe` first, so it stops on Recording Error.
- Closing the window hides it to the tray with no balloon, the owner's pick on 9 November 2026: a one time `LetsClip Is Still Running` balloon went, with its `HasSeenTrayHint` setting.
- A hotkey another app holds is said in a balloon at startup too, since a tray start shows no panel. The Save Hotkey row is one button showing the keys, the owner's pick: a click pauses the hotkey and reads Waiting for Keys, every press replaces the keys shown (a slip is simply pressed again, an unusable one says why), and OK or Enter keeps them, Cancel or Escape puts the old ones back (`letsClip.watchKeys`); while picking, Cancel (a red text button) and OK (filled, disabled until the keys are usable) sit left of the keys button. Hiding the window or leaving the page cancels too (`letsClip.stopKeys`), so the saved hotkey always comes back and no key is swallowed later. The Clips Folder row is Use Default Folder (shown only while a folder is picked), a filled grey button showing the folder that opens it, and Change. A new folder takes everything with it (see `Docs/History.md`, Moving The Clips Folder). Delete Originals When Cutting follows, on by default, the owner's pick (`UserSettings.DeleteOriginals`): Save Cut and Export and Save then replace the clip with the cut and send the full clip to the Recycle Bin (since 20 November 2026, `File.Delete` before), along with any original an earlier cut kept; restored from the bin it lands in `.Originals` again, where the editor offers Restore Original; off, the first cut keeps the full clip in `.Originals` for Restore Original. Kept Originals shows their size or None, with a red filled Delete Kept Originals button that asks first (`YesNoDialog.ConfirmAsync`), listing the cut clips that lose their way back by title with their recorded date greyed on the right, in the Games and Apps dialog's outlined table (`YesNoDialog.Clips`, a long title ends in dots, `title-cell`), the owner's pick on 9 November 2026, and deletes every file in `.Originals` (`ClipLibrary.DeleteOriginals`, a file in use stays for the next delete); the cut clips stay as they are. A Discord plan only re-encodes the export, so the clip always keeps its cut at full quality (checked: a Free Export and Save gave a 1920 x 1080 export of 17.5 MB and left the clip a 2560 x 1440 cut). No cut asks first since 20 November 2026, the owner's pick once the full clip goes to the Recycle Bin: the snackbar says where it went (`Saved the cut, the whole clip is in the Recycle Bin`, or `Restore Original brings back the whole clip` while it is kept). The question it replaced (The parts outside the selection are deleted forever, with a Keep Originals in Settings link) went with that link's Storage reveal. A full clip that cannot be recycled (held by another app) stays kept with its cut's place, so Restore Original still works (`ClipLibrary.CutAsync`, logged).

## Layout

- The editor fills the window and ends `--page-bottom` above it (`page-fill`, `fill`, `fill-column`); a dialog table scrolls inside its rounded box (`.mud-table.fit` with a `max-height`). The window is at least 1280 wide (`MinWidth`, the owner's pick, the page then 1264 px) wherever the screen allows; on a screen narrower than that in effective pixels (a 1080p laptop at 175%, a 1440 wide portrait monitor at 125%) `MainWindow.FitToScreen` lowers the minimum to the work area and zooms the page (`ZoomFactor`), so the same layout just gets smaller and nothing sits off screen, rechecked when the window moves or its DPI changes, so the side panel always sits beside the page; the stacked layout below 1100 px and the icon only navigation below 900 px went with it.
- The clip fills its player edge to edge, the same 24 px from the panel's sides as from its top, the owner's pick, as long as that leaves the controls row room to centre Play: the side panel's width is what the clip or the controls leave, whichever is narrower (`.split.editor` is a size container, `100cqw` less the larger of the clip's width at the height left over, `--clip-aspect` from the probe, and `--controls-min-width`), between 300 and 480 px. `--controls-min-width` is both trim fields, the clock and three icon buttons (`2 × --trim-field-width + 240 px`, 540 px). Without it Play sat 24.5 px right of centre with a landscape clip at 1280 and 1264 x 680 and 87 px with a portrait one; now it is centred at every sweep size, and a landscape clip gets 1.8 px of panel beside it at 1366 x 705 and 24.5 px at 1280 and 1264 x 680, the one place it no longer fills (decided on 9 November 2026, a centred transport over the fill). It counts on the rows under the player keeping fixed heights (`--controls-height` 40 px, `--timeline-height` 72 px). At 1920 x 1009 the side panel is 370 px and a landscape clip fills its 1158 px box; a portrait clip or a very wide window gets the widest side panel and the clip centred. The side panel scrolls like a page (its margins and padding reach into the gutters, its scrollbar sits at the window's edge, `--side-content` is the width inside), so its panels keep their width while it scrolls and their shadows are never clipped.
