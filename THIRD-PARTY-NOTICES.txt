FFmpeg 9.0.2 (n9.0.2-45-g1e65c6cd5d), shared GPL build
https://ffmpeg.org

LetsClip runs ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe as separate programs for screen
capture (ddagrab), encoding, segmenting, probing, thumbnails and exports.

The shipped binaries are the BtbN FFmpeg-Builds asset
ffmpeg-n9.0.2-45-g1e65c6cd5d-win64-gpl-shared-9.0.zip from the release
autobuild-2026-11-03-12-50:
https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/releases/tag/autobuild-2026-11-03-12-50
SHA-256 of the zip: 0d95666ad79ddbbd920860aa06057db39570f7a1efd5bfa9e7606ad4af278e97

It is configured with --enable-gpl --enable-version3 and includes libx264,
libx265 and other GPL components, so this FFmpeg build is licensed under the
GNU General Public License v3.0. Its full licence text ships beside the
binaries as FFmpeg\LICENSE.txt. The complete corresponding source is the FFmpeg
repository at commit 1e65c6cd5d (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg) built with
the scripts at https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds; FFmpeg\README.md in the
LetsClip repository records the same pin.
